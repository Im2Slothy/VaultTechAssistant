RESIDENT ORIENTATION

Let’s get you settled.

  1. Add the assistant.

    Invite the bot to your Discord server. You can also join the community for help and updates.

  2. Open /wasteland.

    Your Pip-Boy menu holds your survivor profile, daily allowance, exploration, companions, fishing, and radio.

  3. Pick your first activity.

    Join a voice channel and select Radio → Tune Station, scavenge for supplies, or begin your own story with /campaign.

RETURNING PLAYER? Most activities now live inside five menus. The command manual shows every current entry point and where to find things.

CHICAGO CAMPAIGN

The Passage Between

A dispatch bell rings above the market. Somewhere across the city, a courier is waiting. Your route through Chicago begins at South Court.

Choose Scrapper, Scout, or Fixer and follow six connected acts through markets, waterways, rooftops, and Breakwater Yard. Inspect the scene, negotiate, solve machinery, or take a tactical approach.

START WITH
/campaign
STRUCTURE
Six acts + optional districts
PROGRESS
Private, saved as you play

Campaign gear and resources are separate from your survivor loadout. Earned survivor rewards transfer only when you explicitly claim them.
