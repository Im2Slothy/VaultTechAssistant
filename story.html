Chicago, after the world changed

People are not cargo.

You arrive at South Court Exchange looking for a way across Chicago’s industrial waterways. Above the market, someone has chalked ROUTES OPEN over an older sign: PEOPLE ARE NOT CARGO.

Then a dispatch bell starts ringing without a hand on it. Somewhere above Spiral Market, a courier is stranded. Downstream, passage has become something people can be charged for, withheld from or forced to earn.

Follow the problem through six connected acts of investigation, practical rescues, conversation and tactical encounters. The city keeps going around you: clinics need medicine, neighbors share walls, and somebody still has a radio show to put on.

Your campaign

One survivor. Your decisions.

A private, finite adventure played through Discord buttons and menus. Choose a background, explore at your pace and return to your saved moment.

This is original fan-made Fallout fiction. The locations, people and events in this guide describe the campaign’s own Chicago.

Your first five minutes

Open Discord. Begin with /campaign.

01 / Open your story

Run /campaign, or choose Start campaign from the bot’s welcome or update panel. Your story opens privately.

02 / Choose a background

Pick Scrapper, Scout or Fixer. Each brings its own campaign SPECIAL scores, equipment and supplies.

03 / Follow the journal

Enter Act I from the Campaign journal. Read the room, choose an action and complete its required district operation to unlock the next act.

If the controls in your server differ, open /help or ask the operator which bot version is running.

Build your approach

Three ways to enter the city.

Scrapper

Strong and durable. Starts with a pipe carbine and patchwork plates. Strength and Endurance are your highest SPECIAL scores.

Scout

Observant and agile. Starts with a hunting carbine and travel leathers. Perception and Agility are your highest scores.

Fixer

Persuasive and technically minded. Starts with a service pistol and reinforced coat. Charisma and Intelligence are your highest scores.

Every background has ordinary noncombat routes through required missions. A failed SPECIAL check leaves another approach.

Keep this close

Your field guide.

Move between areas

Use Field map → Travel to a reached area to revisit safe hubs. From Calumet’s inspection platform, Return to the barge and water-level controls leads back to the valve puzzle. At Clinic Square, Return to the entrance and inspect the dispatch bell reopens the early signal leads. These routes preserve your completed work.

Read, inspect, then act

Use the immediate buttons and action menu. Machinery has readable instructions and warnings in the scene text. Being offered a control is an invitation to think about it.

Make the rescue happen

Supporting a load, stopping a machine and helping a person are separate actions. A promise does not move a passenger. Listen to individual wishes and follow through on the outcome you choose.

Take your time

Enemies respond to your actions. The story does not advance while you are away. Pause mission or Pause encounter saves the room or turn; reopen /campaign to continue after an expired panel.

The tools on your panel
  • Field map: review discovered rooms or travel to reached safe areas. Reviewing a room reads notes; use its travel option to move.
  • Evidence: read collected notes and the source of each account.
  • Armory: check campaign equipment, quest items, HP, AP and supplies; change your field weapon.
  • People: review relationships built through your actions.
  • Records: choose a commendation category, then use Previous commendations and Next commendations to browse its pages. Review your service rank and earned titles here too.

Field tools open from safe mission rooms or the journal. Finish or retreat from an active encounter first.

When a conversation becomes a fight

Watch your campaign HP, Action Points, loaded and reserve ammunition, enemy health and attack warnings. Fire, aimed attacks, cover, reloads, melee and stimpaks have different costs.

Some opponents charge attacks or expose cooling windows. Retreat and defeat leave ways to recover and change your approach; recovery may cost campaign salvage. Use Rest and prepare in the journal for health and a minimum field kit.

If you get stuck at Gary

The landing panel includes Landing guidance matched to your current step. Read the moorings, hear Northwind and ask each occupant separately. A boarding line controls access to a boat; its mooring keeps the boat secured.

Choose an outcome you want to perform. Helping one person, negotiating terms or leaving can close the local story. Nobody has to invent a rescue to keep playing.

Saving, local scenes and restarting

Your actions save as you play. If a panel expires or another interaction supersedes it, run /campaign again.

Some acts enter the original market, barge or landing scenes. Finish that local matter or use Leave local matter unresolved before returning to the mission. The journal offers Resume local scene when one is unfinished.

Restart story asks for confirmation. It replaces your campaign background, equipment, resources and choices. Cancelling keeps progress. Earned commendations and previously claimed survivor rewards remain recorded; restart does not pay those rewards again.

Bring something back

A record of what you actually did.

Investigations, repairs, peaceful solutions, exploration and combat can earn commendations. Open Records to review categories, choose an earned title and see your service record.

Campaign SPECIAL, weapons and mission supplies are separate from your survivor loadout. When eligible, Claim survivor rewards explicitly transfers earned caps and items to your main survivor. Each reward can be paid once per player across story restarts.
