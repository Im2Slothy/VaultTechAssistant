01 / STORY ARC

The story, so far

This is an original story set in the Fallout world, with Chicago at its heart. You arrive as yourself: an established survivor with a history of your own, not a blank slate waiting for someone else to define you.

The opening follows your first encounters with a city full of local routines, difficult choices, and places worth lingering in. You can listen, look closer, ask questions, take another route, or step into a moment that matters to somebody. What you know grows from what you experience.

Beyond the main story, Chicago is full of mysteries, unusual encounters, traces of earlier lives, and details that make a return visit feel different. Follow the leads that interest you and leave the rest for another day.

02 / PLACE & MEMORY

Not a ruin.
A living city.

Chicago carries the shape of everything that came before: streets built for a different future, old gathering places, and stories held in objects whose owners are long gone. But the past is only one layer.

People have made a present here, too. They work, trade, argue, build, remember, celebrate, and make room for one another in ways that are not always tidy. The city should feel varied, specific, and worth discovering, not like an empty backdrop between encounters.

03 / YOUR PLACE IN IT

You are the one
making the difference.

This is your survivor's story. The campaign is being planned around personal history, so your choices belong to your version of Chicago.

01

Keep your history

Your character arrives with the progress and identity you already earned. The narrative is designed to respect the survivor you bring.

02

Choose your approach

Look, listen, talk, investigate, negotiate, explore, or turn back. Ability can open a different path without making one kind of character the only right one.

03

Make choices personal

Relationships and lasting story decisions are intended to shape your own campaign, not overwrite another player's world.

04 / A STORY YOU CAN TOUCH

Read the room.
Then decide.

Discord is the window into the world, not a reason to make the world feel small. The experience is being designed as a sequence of clear, active choices you can follow at your own pace.

  1. 01

    Travel with a sense of place

    Know where you are, what is nearby, and what a journey may ask of you before you commit to it.

    ⌖
  2. 02

    Explore at your own pace

    Move through neighborhoods and interiors, notice the small details, and decide which leads deserve your attention.

    ⌕
  3. 03

    Use the place around you

    When a scene supports it, interact with objects and systems through focused choices: inspect a console, read what is there, or try a connected action.

    ▤
  4. 04

    Leave and come back

    Pause when you need to. A personal campaign should remember what you actually did, and let you return without the world making choices in your absence.

    ↩
