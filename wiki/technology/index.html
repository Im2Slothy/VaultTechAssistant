Equipment & Field Tools

Campaign loadouts, SPECIAL, maps, evidence, service records and the explicit rewards you can bring back to your survivor.

Your field kit

Campaign gear belongs to this adventure.

Your background sets campaign SPECIAL, HP, armor and a starting weapon. Mission ammunition, stimpaks, salvage and quest items belong to the campaign save. They do not draw from your survivor inventory.

Open Armory from a safe room or the journal to inspect equipment and switch between available field weapons. The carbine is balanced, the pistol trades damage for faster AP recovery and the shotgun hits harder with slower AP recovery. Switching shares the existing ammunition pool; it does not refill it.

Health, action points & ammunition

Read your resources before acting.

HP is health. AP means Action Points spent on combat actions. Ammunition is shown as loaded rounds plus reserve. Firing and reloading serve different purposes; a weapon with an empty magazine still has other options.

Strength, Perception, Endurance, Charisma, Intelligence, Agility and Luck make up SPECIAL. Marked checks can offer an efficient route or supplies, but a failed check leaves another way through required missions.

Retreat and defeat preserve recovery paths. Salvage can be spent recovering. Rest at the journal restores health and minimum field supplies without becoming a salvage reward.

Clues & local fixtures

A plate is not a route map.

Brass berth plate

In the original market scene, Tern’s plate identifies a berth. Seeing the number, being permitted to share it and physically owning the plate are different things.

Ledgers & records

Sable’s duplicate ledger, the Calumet orders and later dispatch evidence are accounts to examine and cross-reference. Evidence does not grant permission to publish private names.

Local machinery

Supports, pins, rails and operating bars are explained where they are used. Read the textual sequence; necessary clues remain available without reading an image.

Service record

Commendations follow you across runs.

Commendations recognize story progress, bosses, combat, puzzles, exploration, peaceful choices, people and secrets. Earn points toward service rank and choose an unlocked title in Records.

Claim survivor rewards pays eligible earned caps and items to your main survivor. Payment is recorded once per player, so reopening a room or restarting the story cannot claim the same reward twice.

Restarting resets the campaign run, including build, resources and choices. Earned commendations and claim history remain. A veteran’s campaign cache, when eligible, has its own once-per-run limit.
