Act 1 / South Court Exchange

The Price of a Passage

A ringing dispatch plate leads into a market argument about who gets to travel. Investigate the clinic’s missing medicine and find your footing in the Exchange.

Open mission guidance and local objectives

The clinic case is the required district operation before departure. Inspect the delivery and physical tracks; the signed slip alone does not settle the accusation. Complete the medicine case, then return to the act’s departure.

The optional original market scene concerns Tern and a brass berth plate. Leaving it unresolved preserves that outcome.

Act 2 / Spiral Market

The City Above the Street

Climb a freight tower, work out how to share its limited power and help a stranded courier. The theater and the streets below have problems of their own.

Open mission guidance and local objectives

The tower has infiltration and combat approaches. Read the distribution panel, plan the lift’s power and free Sable from the rack before performing the evacuation. Restoring power alone does not release her.

The theater operation adds a separate worker rescue. Other leads include the projection booth and roof garden.

Act 3 / Calumet

The Water Keeps Accounts

Board a grounded barge, hear a witness and inspect contradictory water orders. Look beyond a stamp or an accusation before deciding what happened.

Open mission guidance and local objectives

The original barge scene separates stabilizing cargo, freeing Mako and hearing his account. Returning without his testimony does not invent it.

The wider act includes pressure controls, a service-float rescue, a signal cabinet and evidence about conflicting water instructions. Read the posted limits before operating controls.

Act 4 / Ash Day Relay

Ash Day

An old evacuation message is sending people toward yesterday’s safety. Repair the signal, check a refuge and help the people who never heard the correction.

Open mission guidance and local objectives

The relay message is genuine but outdated. Diagnose the frequency, ground the system and inspect a destination before carrying out the group’s evacuation.

The laundry operation is a further required task: inspect the loading lane, safely stop its pump and practice an understandable signal. Correcting a broadcast does not move residents who missed it.

Act 5 / Gary Shore Landing

The Names Beneath the Wind

Two boats, separate wishes and a difficult negotiation. Beyond the landing, a shared stove, a radio booth and a public message kiosk keep the shore alive.

Open mission guidance and local objectives

The landing supports individual crossings, a bounded agreement, a launch outcome or an unresolved close. Listen to both occupants. A passage agreement does not mean anyone has crossed.

Shore Square adds a required shared-stove dispute. Optional visits include Deke’s Shore Service booth, Jun’s public message kiosk and the Lakefront Archive. Mara’s mystery stays optional.

Act 6 / Breakwater Yard

The Passage Between

Reach a guarded industrial yard, help its workers and deal with the machinery that enforces its toll. What remains is an arrangement you must put into practice.

Open mission guidance and local objectives

Prepare the yard by rescuing its workers from the service block. Machinery isolation, airflow and the manual exit are separate parts of that operation.

Reach the drive cabinet or confront the Breakwater Engine. The gate must be safely supported before releasing its linkage. After opening it, perform an arrangement rather than finishing with a speech.

Finale spoilers

Three ways to leave the yard.

Public passage

Post checked hazard information with household names removed. Two willing workers begin the present operating period, and the gate stays physically secured open. This establishes one local service, with future maintenance still somebody’s real responsibility.

Bounded service

The present Tinwake watch must accept optional guidance, a visible price, no forced labor or seizure, an open gate and a practical refusal route. A traveler actually declines. Earlier willing Gary crossings must have been performed; if needed, return to resolve them.

Independent exit

Remove the stop-gate coupling, store it and post a hazard warning. The local mechanism can no longer enforce its toll. Its staffed guidance service also ends, and the open water does not become safe by decree.

Optional discoveries

Make time for the side streets.

Undercity Lines, Drowned Stockyards and Lakefront Archive carry optional puzzles, evidence and encounters through multiple acts. Smaller city stories include shared-window repairs, a strange clock, a theater, rooftop weather, radio corrections and a delivery that respects the recipient’s boundaries.
