MAIN STORY · OPENING

A first foothold

The opening teaches the rhythm of exploration: arrive, get your bearings, hear more than one perspective, and decide how much to take part in a local matter. The city is introduced through places people use and the choices they make there.

The survivor is the one who acts. Listening, investigating, leaving, and returning are all meaningful ways to meet the opening on your own terms.

!A day that becomes an occasionStory thread 02 · spoiler-light overviewOpen file

MAIN STORY · COMMUNITY LIFE

A day that becomes an occasion

A neighborhood gathering turns ordinary preparation into something people will remember. Residents bring their own reasons for being there. You can choose a role without taking the event away from the people who made it.

This story finds its scale in a shared moment and what it takes to make room for everyone who wants to take part.

!The long way throughStory thread 03 · spoiler-light overviewOpen file

MAIN STORY · EXPEDITION

The long way through

A substantial expedition asks you to navigate the ground between destinations, notice a landscape that differs from the familiar streets, and decide how far to go. Reaching a place and completing the return are separate parts of the journey.

The story leaves room to explore, pause, change course, and make your own account of what you saw.

!A place held in memoryStory thread 04 · spoiler-light overviewOpen file

MAIN STORY · PLACE & MEMORY

A place held in memory

A hands-on activity offers a small-scale way to explore how people carry a place with them. The memory belongs to the person who offers it; your choices shape how the group engages with it.

Different ways of taking part come with different practical considerations. No single format speaks for the whole city.

!A return with more to considerStory thread 05 · spoiler-light overviewOpen file

MAIN STORY · INTERIOR EXPLORATION

A return with more to consider

An interior visit can change how a familiar route feels. You choose whether to investigate, what kind of help to seek, and when to head back. Exploration and retreat both remain part of your story.

The encounter is built around what you personally see and do, with outcomes tied to the actions you actually take.

!Making a connection across distanceStory thread 06 · spoiler-light overviewOpen file

MAIN STORY · COMMUNICATION

Making a connection across distance

Exploration brings a live connection with people beyond the nearest streets. The story asks how travel, conversation, and direct observation give you your own perspective on a distant community.

What you choose to share remains yours to decide.

!Hospitality is part of the storyStory thread 07 · spoiler-light overviewOpen file

MAIN STORY · COMMUNITY LIFE

Hospitality is part of the story

A shared meal makes room for conversation, practical help, rest, and attention to the people around the table. The hosts keep their own say in what happens; your part is something you choose together.

Not every consequential scene needs danger. Some stories are about being present and making time for one another.
