Character file

Mara Venn

Mara offers an invitation at South Court: a list of Chicago places stranger than their stories, and a choice about whether to travel together.

You may invite her as a story contact or continue alone. She is separate from the bot’s recruited companions and is never required to finish the six acts. Her later presence and conversations depend on your actual choices.

Meeting her at the public notice table does not make her your guide. She can refuse a later request.

Optional character spoilers

Accounts are not the same as absolution.

Open the Tinwake history

At Calumet, Mara can recognize an old crew’s double-loop hitch. At Gary, Northwind identifies herself as Nessa and gives a firsthand account of being left on deck during a mutiny. That does not establish Mara’s motive.

If Mara is present and willing, she can give her own account: she took a child and ran, believed Nessa dead, and did not check. Your response can record belief, doubt or uncertainty, or leave the confession without a reply. It does not forgive Nessa’s injury on her behalf.

The optional Archive can revisit carried evidence. Conversations require the relevant people, testimony and permission; the mystery never blocks a solo campaign.
