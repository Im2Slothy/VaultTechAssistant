People of the Passage

Meet the campaign’s witnesses, workers, travelers and optional story contacts. Character notes describe their role without exposing every revelation.

People you can meet

Everyone has their own part.

Read at your own pace.

The story guide keeps the plot light. Individual files below contain named encounters, puzzle guidance and optional ending spoilers. Open a file to choose how much to see.

Inez Brann

A South Court contact concerned with families, public approaches and the clinic’s work. Her notice can lead you into the original market scene.

Tern

A person at South Court’s public board holding a brass plate. Their plate and position depend on your performed market choices.

Sable Orr

A courier stranded above Spiral Market, carrying pump seals and a duplicate ledger. The freight-lift rescue begins with listening to her.

Mako

Pinned beneath shifted barge cargo at Calumet. His testimony describes what he personally witnessed.

Veda

A runner organizing help for the laundry residents at Ash Day. A route needs to be checked before it can become a plan.

Dara and her sister

People at Gary with their own answers about a launch and the boats. Speak to each person the plan actually involves.

Northwind

The Tinwake figure negotiating at Gary. Her account of the water hazards matters, and it does not settle the fairness of the toll.

Mara Venn

An optional story contact first met at South Court. Her invitation and past can be explored without joining the main recruited-companion roster.

Esme

A forewoman at Breakwater Yard who knows the service block and has tools for its workers’ rescue.

Life around the shore

Bea’s stove, Deke’s radio booth and Jun’s public message kiosk offer problems beyond the landing’s confrontation.

People panel

Trust is earned through actions.

The campaign records individual relationships in People. Helpful choices can build trust and lead to later conversations. A relationship note does not summon a character, move them offscreen or make their decisions for them.
