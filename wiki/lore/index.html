History & Lore

Read the passage conflict, the limits of in-world testimony and the scope of this original Chicago story.

The story’s foundations

Original fiction. Specific witnesses.

The Passage Between asks a practical question: when a route is dangerous, who gets to turn knowledge of it into authority over someone else?

The six acts approach that question through a market, a freight tower, water controls, a relay, two boats and a loaded gate. The work is tangible: inspect, repair, listen, escort and verify what actually changed.

The campaign’s original communities, machinery histories and named characters are fan-made fiction. This archive describes the implemented adventure rather than promoting unused concepts from the private writing archive.

How to read the evidence

Separate what people know from what they believe.

Witnesses and attribution

Mako can tell you what he personally saw. Tern can explain a berth mark. Nessa can describe being left on deck. Each account has a source and a limit. Recording an account does not make its speaker know someone else’s motive.

Names, consent and public records

The campaign lets evidence support action without treating vulnerable people’s names as a public resource. Sable’s ledger, survey records, message delivery and the final hazard board all ask what information may be carried or shared.

Each person involved in a passage or launch keeps a separate answer. A machine shutdown cannot give that answer for them.

The scale of the ending

Breakwater’s three endings change a local mechanism and its present arrangement. They do not make every waterway safe, settle every loss or solve Chicago’s future. Optional aftermath scenes reflect the work recorded in your save.

What this archive covers

The current six-act adventure.

The guide covers the campaign route, expanded local operations, three optional districts, tactical encounters, field tools, commendations and survivor reward claims present in the bot source. Future discovery concepts and broader proposed campaigns are outside this archive.

The deployed bot can differ while an operator is updating. Use /campaign and the controls shown in your server as the entry point.
