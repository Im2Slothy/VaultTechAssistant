Encounters & Machines

A campaign encounter reference for hostile crews, industrial machines and practical alternatives to fighting.

Combat & alternatives

Know what is in front of you.

Read at your own pace.

The story guide keeps the plot light. Individual files below contain named encounters, puzzle guidance and optional ending spoilers. Open a file to choose how much to see.

Hostile crews

Collectors, raiders, ambushers and response teams occupy different parts of the route. Cover, focus fire and attack warnings matter. Required missions retain noncombat approaches.

Machines

Survey and defense drones, turrets and industrial security systems follow their own local instructions. Read the surrounding controls before choosing a fight.

Crawlers

Sump and pipe crawlers appear around industrial water routes. Campaign encounters use your campaign loadout and supplies.

Optional encounter spoilers

Three major machines.

Lockkeeper

An industrial encounter connected to the campaign’s local routes. Watch its announced attack pattern, health phases and openings. Withdrawal keeps recovery possible.

Foundry Warden

The Drowned Stockyards’ old safety machine remains bound to its furnace watch. Fight it or inspect the foundry controls and bank the furnace in the supported order. Its history is revealed through local records.

Breakwater Engine

The yard’s security engine enforces PASSAGE HOLD. Fighting is one route; the local drive cabinet also supplies a manual isolation procedure. Defeating or parking it does not by itself secure the loaded gate or choose the final arrangement.

Practical guidance

Read the next attack.

Combat is turn based. Time away does not let an enemy act. Use cover, reloads, aimed attacks and healing deliberately, then return to the mission after the fight or recovery. Rest and prepare in the journal restores campaign health and a minimum kit.
