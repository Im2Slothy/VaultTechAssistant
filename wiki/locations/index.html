Places on the Passage

From South Court’s stalls to the Breakwater gate, with optional routes beneath the street and along the lake.

Your route

Six places on the passage.

South Court Exchange

Act 1: The Price of a Passage. A ringing dispatch plate leads into a market argument about who gets to travel. Investigate the clinic’s missing medicine and find your footing in the Exchange.

Spiral Market

Calumet

Beyond the main route

Three optional districts.

Read at your own pace.

The story guide keeps the plot light. Individual files below contain named encounters, puzzle guidance and optional ending spoilers. Open a file to choose how much to see.

Undercity Lines / Acts I–II

Entrances appear at South Court’s clinic square and through market leads. Explore Cinder Junction and Quarry Street, solve rail-point and power-board puzzles and investigate the reported 3:10 train. A standoff with collectors offers more than one approach.

Work completed below South Court can open a service stair into Spiral Market. Discoveries and repairs remain recorded when you return.

Drowned Stockyards / Acts III–IV

Follow the route behind Calumet into the flooded Calloway Union Stock & Iron yards. Drain access with sluice controls, inspect the foundry records and return from Ash Day to confront the Foundry Warden.

The Warden has combat and furnace-control approaches. What you learn or carry away can matter again at Breakwater Yard.

Lakefront Archive / Acts V–VI and aftermath

From Gary’s Shore Square, follow the lamp toward the half-sunken pier. Survey tags, damaged decking and an old shipping museum lead to a card catalogue.

The deeper records vault is reached from the Breakwater side. Its cross-references depend on evidence you actually collected. After the performed six-act ending, epilogue rooms can reflect your saved choices.

Setting notes

Chicago, as this story imagines it.

The route connects original campaign locations in a post-apocalyptic Chicago region. It is a fictional play map, not directions for visiting real tunnels, waterways or buildings.
