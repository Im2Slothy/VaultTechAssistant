Chicago Story Guide

OVERVIEW

The Chicago story for Vault-Tec Assistant takes place in 2283. The player arrives as the protagonist, bringing the survivor and progression they have already made their own.

The city is not an empty ruin between missions. Its neighborhoods, public spaces, buildings, and lakefront offer different ways to meet people, explore, find small mysteries, and return to places with a little more context.

Geography

Chicago's current community-area map is a research reference for relative location. It helps keep real-world place names in the right part of the city, while the campaign map is authored separately. Present-day boundaries do not define fictional communities or travel access in 2283.

The Calumet region and Gary are part of the wider Great Lakes research horizon. Each would be a distinct destination with its own people, natural and built landscape, route, and return journey.

Exploration

The Chicago Story Wiki links character profiles, locations, stories, communities, creatures, technology, and history as those articles are prepared. A spoiler warning appears before story details.

Story continuity

The events, characters, and surviving locations created for Chicago in 2283 are original fan fiction, not established Fallout canon.
