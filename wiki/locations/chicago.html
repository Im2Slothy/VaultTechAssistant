Chicago

The fictional setting of The Passage Between, built around the lives and routes of its original six-act adventure.

Original campaign setting

A working city between broken routes.

The Passage Between imagines a Chicago where people still trade, repair machinery, guide travelers and argue about the price of help.

The main route begins at South Court Exchange, rises into Spiral Market’s freight tower, continues through Calumet and Ash Day Relay, and reaches Gary’s shore and Breakwater Yard. Each place has a practical problem, people with partial knowledge and a larger connection to the passage system.

Optional districts connect transit tunnels, flooded stockyards and a lakefront records archive to the evidence you carry. These are authored fictional places and routes for the adventure.

What belongs to this campaign

South Court Exchange, Spiral Market, the Tinwake and the people in these files are original fan-project fiction. Their histories do not establish official Fallout canon or real Chicago geography. Names borrowed from the wider region are used within that fictional setting.

What your map records

The field map grows from reached rooms and safe areas. It preserves discoveries and completed work. Reviewing a room reads your recorded inspection; choosing travel moves you to an eligible reached area.
