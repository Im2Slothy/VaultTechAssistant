The story guide keeps the plot light. Individual files below contain named encounters, puzzle guidance and optional ending spoilers. Open a file to choose how much to see.

The Tinwake

A passage crew whose knowledge of water hazards has become leverage over travelers. At Gary, Northwind describes dangers that are real while the story leaves the terms of the toll open to challenge. Individual crew members and passengers make separate decisions.

In the finale, a bounded service can be performed only with present accepted limits, optional guidance and a practical refusal route. It does not erase earlier harms.

The Long Shift

A local watchkeeper appears at Ash Day among the waiting travelers. Their participation in a refuge depends on an actual handoff and accepted watch. The campaign does not treat one local person as an agreement from a whole faction.

The Free Interval

A local guide at Ash Day offers an inspected route toward an open tram shelter. The destination has limits, including no stocked water. Guidance and an escort need to happen in the scene.

The people who keep a place alive

Clinic workers, loading runners, stall owners, theater workers, laundry residents, shore neighbors and yard crews have local needs. Solving their cases can change relationships, evidence and later options.

Listen to the particular person who must accept a plan. Consent from a representative, a stamp or an operator is never a substitute for the passenger’s own choice.
