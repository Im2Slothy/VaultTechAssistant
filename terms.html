2. Description of Service

The Bot provides Fallout-themed entertainment and utility features for Discord servers. These features include, but are not limited to: a persistent player-based economy with items and bottlecaps; interactive minigames (terminal hacking, lockpicking, gambling); a crafting, selling, and player-vending system; player-to-player item trading; a long-term expedition system; a player-run settlement management system; PVE encounters and boss battles; and 24/7 playback of simulated Fallout-themed radio stations. Features may be added, modified, or removed at the discretion of the Bot developer(s).

The private Chicago campaign provides a six-act Chicago RPG campaign with exploration, optional character conversations, campaign field kits and SPECIAL checks, turn-based combat, noncombat alternatives, illustrations, and a saved local ending. Each player's history and campaign resources are separate. Campaign HP, AP, ammunition, supplies, and equipment are separate from existing survivor gameplay. The explicit Claim survivor rewards action can pay earned caps, items, and profile titles into your regular survivor account. Rewards are limited to one claim per player, including across story restarts. The larger planned discovery library, random encounter generation, and other future story content are not included unless specifically announced as available.

Restarting the campaign requires confirmation and replaces the active campaign save. Persistent commendations, titles, and survivor reward claim records are retained. This cannot be undone through the Bot. The Bot does not guarantee recovery of replaced or deleted saves.
